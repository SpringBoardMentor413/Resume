BRIGHTDESK — TECHNICAL SUPPORT ESCALATION POLICY

SCOPE OF THIS QUEUE
This queue is for TECHNICAL / PRODUCT issues only — things that are broken, not working,
or behaving unexpectedly in the BrightDesk application. Billing questions, invoice disputes,
refund requests, and subscription cancellations belong to the Billing team, not this queue.
If a ticket is purely a billing matter, it should be flagged for redirect to Billing rather
than assigned a technical priority tier.

TIER 1 — ESCALATE NOW (respond within 1 hour)
A ticket belongs here if it describes any of the following, regardless of how it is worded:
- A complete service outage affecting multiple customers (the product is down/unreachable)
- Data loss or data corruption affecting a customer's account or records
- A payment processing failure that is currently preventing a customer from completing a
  transaction (this is a technical processing failure, not a billing dispute — it belongs
  in this queue)
- A security vulnerability, suspected breach, or unauthorized account access
- A ticket that was previously marked resolved and is reopened within 48 hours of that
  resolution, with the customer reporting the same underlying issue is still broken

TIER 2 — SAME DAY (respond within 8 business hours)
A ticket belongs here if it describes:
- A specific feature or integration broken for one individual customer, where a workaround
  exists or the impact is limited to that one account
- A login or access issue affecting a single customer account (not a broad outage)
- A compliance-related data request (e.g., a data export or deletion request) that has a
  regulatory response window

TIER 3 — CAN WAIT (respond within 2 business days)
A ticket belongs here if it describes:
- A cosmetic issue (colors, text, layout) that does not affect functionality
- A feature request or product suggestion
- A general how-to question that existing help documentation likely answers
- General positive feedback or a thank-you note with no action required

HANDLING AMBIGUOUS OR INCOMPLETE TICKETS
If a ticket does not contain enough information to determine severity (e.g., "something's
wrong, please help" with no further detail), do not guess a tier. Flag it as
"needs more information — request details from customer before triaging."

HANDLING BORDERLINE CASES
If a ticket is close to a stated threshold above (for example, near the 48-hour reopen
window, or the timestamp is unclear) but not clearly on one side of it, do not force it into
a tier. Flag it as "borderline — confirm with team lead" and state exactly what is
ambiguous.

WHAT MUST NOT INFLUENCE TIER ASSIGNMENT
Tier assignment must be based only on the technical severity and business impact described
above. The following must never raise or lower a ticket's tier, and must not be mentioned in
the reasoning for a ticket's placement:
- The customer's age, or anything that implies it
- Disability status or any accommodation request mentioned in the ticket
- Family circumstances, personal emergencies, or other life events mentioned in the ticket
- The customer's English fluency, phrasing, grammar, or writing style
- The tone or emotional intensity of the ticket (e.g., all-caps, exclamation points, or
  conversely an unusually calm or brief description) — judge only the substance of what is
  reported as broken or blocked
