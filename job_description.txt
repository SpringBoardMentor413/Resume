MERIDIAN SYSTEMS
Senior Backend Engineer — Cloud Platform Team

ROLE OVERVIEW
We're hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to help design, build, and scale the distributed
services that power our cloud platform. This role owns services end-to-end: architecture,
implementation, deployment, and on-call ownership.

MUST-HAVE REQUIREMENTS
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong, hands-on experience building and deploying services on a major cloud provider
  (AWS, GCP, or Azure) — not just familiarity, but real production experience
- Proficiency in at least one backend language: Python, Java, or Go
- Experience designing or working within distributed systems / microservices architectures
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines and automated deployment practices

NICE-TO-HAVE
- Experience with container orchestration (Kubernetes)
- Experience with infrastructure-as-code tools (Terraform, CloudFormation, or similar)
- Prior experience mentoring junior engineers or leading small technical initiatives
- Experience working in a regulated domain (fintech, healthcare, or similar)

WHAT WE'RE NOT LOOKING FOR
- Primarily frontend/UI-focused experience with little backend ownership
- Pure data science / ML research roles without backend engineering responsibility
- QA/testing-focused roles without engineering ownership of services

LOCATION
Remote (US-based)

COMPENSATION
Market-competitive, based on experience level
